# Kora generation constraints

Read the catalog and load the actual reference images. Text-only recall does not establish visual identity. Do not generate or redesign anything merely because this guide is present.

Kora is an independent small office koala in Cora's creative universe, not a literal animal avatar of Cora. Keep the exact approved facial identity, eye size and spacing, broad nose proportions, large fluffy ears, fur distribution, apparent age and compact body silhouette. KOR-ID-001 is the primary facial identity reference. Reuse frozen approved views when available. New views must refer directly to the primary identity, not a chain of generated scenes.

Use references by scope: KOR-WD-002 for the approved muted blue hoodie and clean matte material; KOR-KM-001 for the locked left-side waist placement; KOR-KM-002/003 for side/rear-left visibility; approved expression and action images only for those respective behaviors. A scene image is not a replacement identity master. Legacy view filenames use inconsistent screen-facing directions: determine anatomical side from the image and body orientation, never from the filename alone.

Exactly one small handwritten K exists as natural darker pigmentation within the fur on Kora's anatomical LEFT lateral waist, below the ribs and above the hip. Never on the right, belly center, back, shoulder, ear, limb or clothing. Target height 8–10% of visible torso height; target pigmentation 12–18% darker than neighboring fur under neutral light. These are design targets, not measurements certified from the reference raster. Same hair length, texture, direction and depth; softly feathered edges. No ink, tattoo, decal, embroidery, shave, scar, print, black graphic or decorative flourish. Respect foreshortening and occlusion. Do not enlarge or move the K for visibility. Clothing hides it completely.

Rear views primarily show the posterior furry ear surfaces. The inner pink bowls cannot stay camera-facing when the skull turns away. Preserve compact koala limbs and supported seated poses. No externally protruding tail under the established rule. Two saved unclothed nine-view sheets have an unresolved lower-back protrusion conflict and are withheld from the default package; do not use them to introduce a tail.

Keep expression quiet and restrained. A subtle smile means a minimal mouth-corner arc; no teeth, wide grin, exaggerated anger, wrinkles, eye bags, blush or sparkling enlarged eyes. Clothing: muted blue hoodie, hood down, two short drawstrings, one minimal eucalyptus leaf graphic. Do not reproduce the natural K on clothing.

Preserve approved source colors and task lighting. Historical cool-neutral and warm-neutral wording has not been reconciled into a measured palette; do not recolor originals or invent HEX values. No final Graphic K logo is authorized. Never invent biography, demographic attributes, ownership permissions or publication approval.

If a required reference is missing, identify the missing asset ID and ask for the image or access; do not substitute a generic koala. New results are candidates until Cora explicitly approves their intended scope.
